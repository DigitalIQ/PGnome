    PGNome

    A small island. A handful of Nomes. Every child inherits a mix of its parents' genes, with small mutations.

    • Nomes eat plants, look for a partner and try to stay comfortable.
    • Seasons change the temperature. Winter is lean. Big Nomes keep warm, big ears help in heat.
    • Weather, day and night shape life. Hawks hunt by day, seaweed grows in the shallows, moonflowers open at night.
    • Islands: a main island and smaller ones nearby. Only swimmers reach them.
    • You shape the land, carry Nomes and drop food. You never control them directly.
